FAQ

Before you decide — good answers

Is this really a one-time payment?
+
Yes. You pay once and own ALINEA Pro forever. No subscriptions, no renewals. Every future update is included in your purchase. The price will rise to $69 USD after the Early Bird period ends.
How do I install it? Is it an app?
+
ALINEA is a single HTML file — no App Store, no Play Store, no installation required. Open it in any browser on your phone, tablet, Mac or PC. You can also add it to your home screen as a PWA in under 3 seconds.
Is my data safe? Where is it stored?
+
Completely safe — because it never leaves your device. ALINEA has no servers, no cloud, no accounts. Everything lives in your browser's localStorage. No one can access, track, or sell your reading data. Not even us.
What's the difference between Beta and Pro?
+
Beta lets you experience ALINEA fully with up to 3 books, at no cost. Pro removes all limits: unlimited books, full color palette with custom hex codes, Advanced Bitácora with missions and Top 5, PDF export, and all future AI features.
What happens if I lose the file?
+
Your purchase gives you permanent access to download ALINEA again from Lemon Squeezy anytime. We recommend keeping a copy of the file in a safe place on your device or cloud storage of your choice.
