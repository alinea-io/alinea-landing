Does ALINEA work on my device?
+
Yes — iPhone, Android, desktop and tablet. We recommend installing it as a WebApp (PWA): on iPhone tap Share → "Add to Home Screen", on Android tap the menu → "Install app". It works just like a native app, no App Store needed. Since ALINEA is local-first, the only limit on books and notes is your device's available storage.
How do I download ALINEA Beta?
+
Beta is completely free. Go to the checkout page, enter your email — it's $0, no card required. You'll get a confirmation email with the link to open ALINEA directly in your browser. Install it as an app from there and you're ready to go.
Is ALINEA really a one-time purchase?
+
Yes. Buy it once and keep it forever. No subscriptions, no renewals. Every future update arrives on its own — nothing to install, nothing to renew.
How do I get ALINEA Pro?
+
Click "Get ALINEA Pro" and you'll go to the checkout. Pay once via Lemon Squeezy — secure and instant. You'll receive a license key by email. Open ALINEA, go to Settings, enter your key and your full galaxy unlocks immediately.
Where is my data stored?
+
Your library lives entirely on your device. ALINEA has no servers. No one can access or track your data — not even us.
Why are there no subscriptions?
+
Knowledge shouldn't feel rented. Buy ALINEA once and keep your universe forever.
Can I import highlights from Kindle or Goodreads?
+
Not yet — but it's coming. Kindle and Goodreads import is on the roadmap for ALINEA Pro. For now, you can add your highlights manually or by dictation directly inside the app.
Why doesn't ALINEA require an account?
+
Because your reading life belongs to you — not to a platform. Your purchase generates a unique key that unlocks your local database.
Can I back up my library?
+
Yes. You can export your entire galaxy as JSON anytime. We recommend creating regular backups of your library.
What happens to my data if I stop using ALINEA?
+
Nothing changes. Your data lives on your device — not on our servers. If you stop using ALINEA, everything stays exactly where you left it. Your galaxy is yours, forever.
